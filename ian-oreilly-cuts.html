On the approach

A cut here starts with your hair, not with a chart on the wall. How it grows, where it splits, what it does on the third day when nobody is watching it. Ian works to that, then builds the shape around it.

Fades kept clean and low-contrast where the head asks for it, sharper where it doesn’t. Texture cut in by hand rather than sprayed on afterwards. You should be able to leave, wash it, and still have the same haircut in the morning.
